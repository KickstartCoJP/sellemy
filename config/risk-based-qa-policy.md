# BU-002 Sellemy Risk-based Product and QA policy (2026-11-25)

The prior comparison acceptance contract remains hash-stable for historical receipts.
This policy governs new and retried articles. Existing successful receipts are
not retroactively revoked merely because review procedure became lighter.

Strong stop: wrong product, unrelated category, demonstrated same-body/color-only
or pack-only duplicate, official recall match, dangerous usage advice, materially
false or unsubstantiated performance, safety, efficacy or compatibility claims.

Fix in place: weak recommendation, repetitive prose, unsupported specific
phrasing, unclear conclusion. Route prose edits to Writer, never restart all six
product research unless an actual product identity/fit failure is evidenced.

Allowed: incomplete optional specification, lack of manufacturer manual,
non-quantified everyday recommendation, missing verbatim identity quotation
where the source-backed listing title already establishes identity, unknown
pairwise duplicate status without positive evidence.

Retain: six real distinct products, three editorial groups of two, sponsored
product exclusions, ratings/review eligibility, independent Product QA,
Editorial QA, material Claim QA, Safety QA, source-linked safety facts, immutable
receipts, and CEO gate before publication.

Collection outage: use a complete government safety snapshot no older than
seven days while recording its staleness; older or incomplete corpora cannot
issue a safety clearance. Daily monitor retries independently, preserving
previous alerts and recording acquisition failure.
