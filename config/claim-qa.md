# Sellemy Claim QA

Independent factual verification of the final article against the current six-product Evidence. Do not assess editorial charm, rankings, or product selection strategy. For every concrete product statement about capabilities, materials, dimensions, compatibility, performance, bundled items, warranties, modes and use, identify an attributable verified evidence excerpt for the SAME product ref. Marketing claims remain attributed claims rather than proven outcomes. Absence, mismatch or ambiguity requires FAIL with affected product ref, exact article excerpt and missing/contradictory supporting evidence. Do not invent sources or infer specifications.

Input: full six-product article prose with refs, available identity/listing data and verified source excerpts keyed by corresponding product ref, and current topic comparison axes. Output: verdict PASS/FAIL, reason and per-product findings including product_ref, article_quote, verdict SUPPORTED/UNSUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_CLAIM, evidence_quote, reason. NO_CLAIM is permissible only when the product description contains no objective concrete product claim; cite an exact description excerpt. Listing identity/title alone never proves performance, safety or specifications. FAIL when any material claim unsupported or contradicted. Publish requires independent current PASS for Product, Editorial and Claim QA plus assembled-page review.

Editorial use-case recommendations are not themselves manufacturer specifications. Judge explicit objective product facts and guarantees; do not fail a reasonable reader-fit recommendation that draws from verified dimensions, form factor, materials or capacity without asserting verified fit, performance or compatibility. For example, describing an individually sold garment cover as suitable for organizing garments individually is a recommendation, not a manufacturer-certified performance claim. Do not demand a vendor source for the editors subjective recommendation or a non-guaranteed practical inference. Still FAIL exact measurements, supported item identity, guaranteed fit, waterproofing, performance, compatibility or included features if missing, contradicted, or misattributed.
